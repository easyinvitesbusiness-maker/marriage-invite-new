Wedding invitation — credits, licences, and how to edit
=======================================================

How to edit the wording
-----------------------
Open script.js and change the CONTENT object at the very top.
That object is the only place you need to touch for:

  • names (couple and parents)
  • date, time and timezone
  • venue name and address (and an optional Google Maps URL)
  • all headlines and invitation wording
  • the hashtag
  • RSVP email and WhatsApp number (these are placeholders)
  • gallery photo paths and captions

Reload the page after you save. The countdown, “Add to Google
Calendar” link, the downloadable .ics file and the Get Directions
link are all rebuilt from CONTENT automatically.

Replace the placeholder photos in assets/gallery/ with your own
and update the CONTENT.gallery list to match.

To change the background music, replace assets/music.mp3 with
another track (keep the same filename, or update the <audio>
tag in index.html).

Illustrations
-------------
The watercolor envelope, roses, floral corners, proposal scene,
flower field, couple portrait, estate, wisteria-and-lanterns
garland, and parent-card artwork were created as original
illustrations for this invitation. They are not copies of a
third-party wedding website.
